**Course** **Description****:**

Linear algebra, vectors and matrices, matrix operations, vector spaces and linear transformations, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, matrix factorizations (LU, QR, SVD), norms and distance measures, multivariate calculus, limits and continuity, differentiation and partial derivatives, gradients and Jacobians, optimization fundamentals, convex sets and convex functions, unconstrained optimization, probability theory, random variables and probability distributions, expectation and variance, joint and conditional distributions, Bayes theorem, statistical inference, sampling methods, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, correlation and covariance, regression fundamentals, multivariate statistics, numerical methods for data science.

**Course Objectives**

|   |   |
|---|---|
|**No**|**Course Objective**|
|**CO1**|**Linear algebra foundations** - To introduce students to the fundamental concepts of linear algebra, including vectors, matrices, vector spaces, and linear transformations, as tools for representing and manipulating data in high-dimensional spaces.|
|**CO2**|**Matrix structure and decomposition techniques** - To develop an understanding of matrix operations and decompositions such as LU, QR, and SVD, and to highlight their role in solving linear systems.|
|**CO3**|**Multivariate Calculus and data modeling -** To provide foundational knowledge of multivariate calculus, including limits, continuity, partial derivatives, gradients, Jacobians, and Hessians, with applications to data-driven modeling and optimization.|
|**CO4**|**Optimization principles in data science** – To introduce the principles of optimization, including convex sets and convex functions, and to develop intuition for unconstrained optimization methods used in machine learning algorithms.|
|**CO5**|**Probability theory and stochastic modeling** – To build a strong foundation in probability theory, including random variables, probability distributions, expectation, variance, and joint and conditional distributions for modeling uncertainty in data.|
|**CO6**|**Statistical inference and data interpretation** - To introduce statistical inference techniques such as sampling methods, estimation, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing for drawing conclusions from the data.|
|**CO7**|**Numerical and statistical methods for computational data science** – To expose students to numerical methods and multivariate statistical techniques, including regression, covariance analysis, and computational approaches for solving real-world data science problems.|

**Objective of Experiential Learning Component:**

Hands on sessions on the following:

a) Vectors and Matrices: create vectors and matrices in NumPy/Octave and solve for Ax=b.

b) Geometry of vector spaces – compute projections, construct different bases.

c) Eigenvalues, eigenvectors and Principal Components Analysis – compute eigenvalues and eigenvectors, perform PCA manually and compare with SciKit-Learn PCA.

d) Compute SVD and low-rank approximation of a matrix. Observe compression effects on an image.

e) Multivariate Calculus and optimization – numerically approximate derivatives and compare with analytical derivatives.

f) Gradient descent – implement gradient descent for simple functions.

g) Probability and Monte-Carlo simulation.

h) Bayesian reasoning

Confidence intervals and hypothesis testing
