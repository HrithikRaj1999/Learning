# Heap — Two Heaps

Balance a max-heap (low half) against a min-heap (high half).

| LC# | Diff | Question | Pattern | Companies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 295 | Hard | Find Median from Data Stream | Max-heap + min-heap balanced | Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Uber |
| 502 | Hard | IPO | Max-heap of affordable profits | Amazon, Google |
| 264 | Medium | Ugly Number II | Min-heap / 3-pointer DP | Amazon, Bloomberg |

**Key skill**: keep heaps balanced (sizes differ ≤1); median is a top or the average of both tops (LC 295).
