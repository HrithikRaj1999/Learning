# DP — 1D

State depends on the previous one or two indices.

| LC# | Diff | Question | Pattern | Companies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70 | Easy | Climbing Stairs | dp[i]=dp[i-1]+dp[i-2] | Amazon, Google, Adobe |
| 746 | Easy | Min Cost Climbing Stairs | Min of two prior + cost | Amazon, Google |
| 198 | Medium | House Robber | rob = max(skip, prev2 + cur) | Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft |
| 213 | Medium | House Robber II | Rob two linear ranges (circular) | Amazon, Google, Microsoft |
| 91 | Medium | Decode Ways | dp on 1-digit / 2-digit splits | Amazon, Meta, Microsoft |

**Key skill**: most 1D DP compresses to two rolling variables — O(1) space (LC 70, 198).
