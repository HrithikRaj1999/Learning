## Frequency observations

```text
- Google asks the most overall (Two Sum ~158x in 6 months).
- Amazon is consistently #2 across almost every problem.
- Meta favors: Trees, Sliding Window, Backtracking.
- Microsoft favors: Matrix, Binary Search, Linked List.
- Bloomberg loves: Intervals, Stack, BFS/DFS.
- Goldman Sachs focuses: Two Pointers, Math, DP.
- Hard (Trapping Rain Water, Median of Two Sorted Arrays) hits often at senior levels.
```
